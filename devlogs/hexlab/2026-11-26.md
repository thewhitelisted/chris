HexLab has very quickly became both my favourite and largest project. I started it as a way of developing tools for people to get better at Catan, and now it's a lab that builds evaluation engines that play the game better than any human (or any bot) in the world. Unfortunately I am keeping the project private for now until I can release the new version 2 of basalt (basalt is the name of the engine). I'm very excited of the advancements that the second version is bringing, so stay tuned.
